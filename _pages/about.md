Hi, I'm Joo Yun. I study why people don't eat the way they know they should, and how food environments can be designed to make better choices easier.

I hold an M.S. in Clinical Nutrition from Hanyang University and an M.A. in Cognitive Psychology from Sungkyunkwan University, and I was a research assistant at a behavioral economics lab at Cornell University. I have worked as a registered dietitian counseling patients with diabetes and obesity, a sports nutrition coach at Adidas, and a product manager and UX designer on the early team of the food-tech startup Wehuddling. I now run [Nutricious Studio & Lab](https://jooyunha.imweb.me/).

**Research interests:** nutrition information framing · food choice architecture · menu wording and portion size · decision-making in everyday eating

## Research

- **Food concepts and weight status** (Computational Cognition & Development Lab, Hanyang University, 2022 – 2024): an online free-association task with 135 Korean food cues, run with 112 adults, to compare response latency, associative fluency, and cue–response semantic networks between people with obesity and normal-weight people
- **Food color, taste, and intake** (M.A. thesis, Sungkyunkwan University, 2017): a 2 × 2 between-subjects experiment showing that food color shifted predicted intake while taste drove actual intake
- **Supermarket interventions** (Dyson School of Applied Economics and Management, Cornell University, 2017): literature review on interventions for healthier purchases and on-site consumer surveys for a field study on ice cream preference (with Dr. Catalina Velez)
- [CONSUMEHealth](https://giovannisogari.com/consumehealth-msca-project/about-the-project/2-non-categorizzato/64-abstract-of-my-project-korean.html) (EU Horizon 2020 Marie Skłodowska-Curie project, PI: Giovanni Sogari): Korean translation of the project abstract
