Hi, I'm Joo Yun. I study why people don't eat the way they know they should, and how food environments can be designed to make better choices easier.

I hold an M.S. in Clinical Nutrition from Hanyang University and an M.A. in Cognitive Psychology from Sungkyunkwan University, and I was a research assistant at a behavioral economics lab at Cornell University. I have worked as a registered dietitian counseling patients with diabetes and obesity, a sports nutrition coach at Adidas, and a product manager and UX designer on the early team of the food-tech startup Wehuddling. I now run [Nutricious Studio & Lab](https://jooyunha.imweb.me/).

**Research interests:** nutrition information framing · food choice architecture · menu wording and portion size · decision-making in everyday eating
